The idea. Five tabs at the bottom, always in reach: Home (only what needs you today), Houses (where to loot, Dot, route, daily), Map, Bag (pack, stash, workbench, kitchen) and Me (skills, wardrobe, crew & pets, cards, collections, bestiary, capsules, journal).
